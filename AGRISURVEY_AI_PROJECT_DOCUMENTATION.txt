1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PLATFORM OVERVIEW
====================================================================================================

AgriSurvey AI is a state-of-the-art agricultural administration, geospatial crop survey, and remote 
sensing verification platform designed specifically for the land revenue and agricultural ecosystem 
of Maharashtra, India. 

The platform bridges the gap between traditional land revenue records (7/12 extracts, Gat/Survey 
numbers) and modern Earth Observation (EO) technologies by fusing:
- High-resolution European Space Agency (ESA) Sentinel-2 L2A optical satellite imagery via AWS Earth 
  Search STAC APIs.
- Centimeter-accurate drone photogrammetry and georeferenced RTK orthomosaics.
- Geotagged mobile ground surveys with offline-first synchronisation.
- Rigorous WGS84 ellipsoidal geodesic spatial calculations powered by Turf.js.
- Explainable agronomic machine learning (Random Forest crop classification, NDVI timeseries anomaly 
  detection, and yield forecasting baselines).
- A legally sound administrative workflow connecting Farmers, Tahsildars (Revenue Officers), Field 
  Surveyors (Drone Pilots), and State Administrators under the Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, 1966.

AgriSurvey AI operates under a strict "Statutory Separation Principle": AI predictions and machine 
inferences are explicitly tagged as preliminary observations (AI_PREDICTION / PRELIMINARY_AI_OBSERVATION) 
and remain legally isolated from official revenue records until formally reviewed and ratified by a 
gazetted Tahsildar.

2. OFFICIAL PROBLEM STATEMENT & STATUTORY CONTEXT
====================================================================================================

2.1. The Agricultural Reality in Maharashtra
Agricultural crop surveys in Maharashtra are historically manual, time-consuming, labor-intensive, 
and vulnerable to significant delays and recording discrepancies. Over 15 million farmers across 36 
districts (spanning agro-climatic zones from the Konkan coast to Western Ghats, Deccan plateau, and 
Vidarbha/Marathwada drought-prone belts) cultivate diverse seasonal crops such as Sugarcane, Soybean, 
Cotton, Sorghum (Jowar), Pearl Millet (Bajra), Wheat, Onions, and Pulses across Kharif, Rabi, and 
Zaid/Summer seasons.

2.2. Critical Deficiencies of Legacy Manual Surveys
Under the legacy manual survey system (administered by village Talathis / Patwaris):
1. Delay & Omission: Physical field visits to millions of fragmented land parcels are practically 
   impossible within statutory timelines, resulting in outdated or unverified crop entries in the 
   official Record of Rights (RoR / 7/12 extracts).
2. Dispute & Fraud: Subsidies, Minimum Support Price (MSP) procurements, and disaster compensation 
   often suffer from inaccurate crop declarations or phantom cultivations.
3. Crop Insurance Bottlenecks: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) insurance claim settlements 
   take months or years due to contested manual Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) and lack of verifiable, 
   timestamped, geotagged evidence.
4. Disaster Relief Delays: When localized hailstorms, unseasonal rains, or droughts strike districts 
   like Pune, Solapur, or Satara, manual panchnamas take weeks, leaving distressed farmers without timely 
   liquidity.
5. Planar Coordinate Distortion: Traditional desktop cadastral tools frequently perform naive planar 
   calculations on raw latitude/longitude degrees, introducing severe geometric and acreage errors 
   at Maharashtra latitudes (~18° to 20° North).

2.3. The Core Mission & Mandate of AgriSurvey AI
AgriSurvey AI delivers an end-to-end, trustworthy, digital-first geospatial system that:
- Establishes a verified digital registry of agricultural parcels with WGS84 geodesic geometry.
- Continuously monitors crop growth, vegetation vigor, and weather patterns using satellite STAC feeds.
- Coordinates drone flight missions for high-resolution aerial ground truthing.
- Provides field workers with offline-resilient mobile data collection tools.
- Empowers Tahsildars with side-by-side comparison workspaces to reconcile farmer declarations, 
  ground photos, drone imagery, and satellite spectral analyses.
- Automates statutory reporting across Village, Taluka, and District tiers in compliance with 
  governmental standards.

3.4. 4-Tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture
The system enforces strict principle-of-least-privilege role boundaries:
1. Farmer / Landholder:
   - Registers land parcels and submits 7/12 revenue documents.
   - Captures and uploads 5-6 mandatory ground photographs with GPS verification.
   - Monitors crop health, weather forecasts, and application progress.
   - Downloads certified plot survey reports and official verification PDFs.
   - Restricted from viewing other farmers' unverified private records or accessing administrative review tools.
2. Tahsildar / Taluk Revenue Officer:
   - Exercises statutory administrative authority over land verification applications within assigned Taluks.
   - Reviews 7/12 extracts, survey numbers, and applicant credentials.
   - Approves or rejects applications (mandatory justification required for rejection).
   - Utilizes the side-by-side Image Comparison Workspace (ground photos vs drone orthomosaics).
   - Reconciles declared crops vs observed crops and confirms or overrides AI classifications.
   - Digitally signs and finalizes statutory verification reports.
3. Field Verification Worker / Drone Pilot:
   - Receives assigned field inspection missions.
   - Captures mobile geotagged field surveys with crop condition, growth stage, and soil observations.
   - Operates in offline mode in remote villages, synchronizing when connectivity is restored.
   - Uploads high-resolution RTK drone imagery and logs flight telemetry.
   - Restricted from approving revenue applications or finalizing statutory reports.
4. System Administrator & State Intelligence Director:
   - Accesses statewide and district-level spatial analytics dashboards.
   - Configures jurisdictional boundaries, staff assignments, and crop health thresholds.
   - Inspects immutable audit logs capturing every user action, state change, and timestamp.
   - Generates and exports regional macro-reports across Village, Taluka, and District levels.

5.1. Module 1: High-Precision GIS Farm Plot Registration & Geodesic Boundary Math
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Establishes the authoritative spatial baseline for every agricultural land parcel in Maharashtra.
- Features:
  * Collects required metadata: Survey Number, Gat Number, Sub-division, Village, Taluk, District, State, 
    Cultivation Season, Survey Date, and 7/12 Land Record Reference.
  * Interactive Polygon Drawing: Surveyors and farmers draw parcel boundaries directly on satellite imagery.
  * Real-Time Validation: Validates polygon closure, detects and rejects self-intersecting geometries (kinks), 
    and checks for coordinate anomalies.
  * Geodesic Ellipsoidal Calculations (Turf.js WGS84 EPSG:4326):
    - Computes area in square meters (m²), hectares (ha), and acres (ac).
    - Computes perimeter in meters (m).
    - Guarantees true ellipsoidal curvature precision, preventing planar degree distortion.
  * Unique Plot Reference Generation: Generates standardized cadastral identifiers (e.g. `MH-PUN-HAV-PLOT-2026-1577`).
- Key Files: `src/services/plot.service.ts`, `src/lib/geospatial.ts`, `src/pages/gis/GisCropSurveyPage.tsx`.

5.2. Module 2: Satellite Imagery Integration (Sentinel-2 L2A STAC Discovery)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Delivers automated, cloud-free Earth Observation imagery for registered agricultural plots.
- Features:
  * Connects to the AWS Earth Search STAC v1.0 Catalog (`https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/search`).
  * Queries collection `sentinel-2-l2a` using plot bounding boxes and dynamic date ranges.
  * Filters scenes by maximum cloud cover threshold (e.g. < 20%).
  * Discovers individual spectral band assets: B02 (Blue), B03 (Green), B04 (Red), B08 (Near-Infrared), 
    B11/B12 (Short-Wave Infrared), and SCL (Scene Classification Layer).
  * Stores scene metadata and footprints in `satellite_scenes` collection.
  * Seamless fallback to verified cached scenes if live external network connectivity is restricted.
- Key Files: `src/services/stac.service.ts`, `src/components/satellite/SatelliteSearchModal.tsx`.

5.3. Module 3: Genuine NDVI Spectral Processing & Canopy Analysis
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Quantifies photosynthetic vegetation vigor and canopy health at parcel resolution.
- Features:
  * Real Spectral Band Algebra: Calculates Normalized Difference Vegetation Index:
    NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)  =>  (B08 - B04) / (B08 + B04)
  * Scientific Boundary Enforcement: Strictly bounded within [-1.0, +1.0].
  * Zero-Denominator Guard: Protects against division-by-zero on black or missing pixels.
  * 5 Vegetation Density Classifications:
    1. Very High Density (NDVI >= 0.60): Dense, healthy, vigorous crop canopy (e.g. mature sugarcane, irrigated crops).
    2. Moderate Density (0.40 <= NDVI < 0.60): Normal vegetative growth, mid-stage crops.
    3. Sparse / Stressed (0.20 <= NDVI < 0.40): Early emergence, moisture stress, or scattered vegetation.
    4. Bare Soil / Tilled (0.05 <= NDVI < 0.20): Fallow land, prepared seedbeds, or harvested fields.
    5. Non-Vegetated / Water (NDVI < 0.05): Water bodies, farm ponds, structures, roads, or rocky outcrops.
  * Dynamic False-Color Raster Generation: Generates georeferenced Canvas data URLs mapped to Leaflet `ImageOverlay`.
  * Multi-Date Temporal Trends: Tracks historical NDVI curves across the cultivation lifecycle.
- Key Files: `src/services/ndvi.service.ts`, `src/components/maps/AgriculturalGISMap.tsx`.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4. Module 4: Geo-Tagged Mobile Field Survey Collection & Offline Sync
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: Equips ground surveyors with an offline-first mobile inspection application for field ground truthing.
- Features:
  * Captures HTML5 Geolocation with high-accuracy GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, ±accuracy in meters).
  * Validates surveyor proximity to the target plot centroid (warns if > 500m away).
  * Records field observations: Observed Crop, Cultivation Season, Growth Stage (Germination, Vegetative, 
    Flowering, Fruiting, Maturity, Harvesting), Soil Moisture Condition (Dry, Moist, Waterlogged, Cracked), 
    and Irrigation Method (Canal, Borewell, Drip, Sprinkler, Rainfed).
  * Ground Evidence Capture: Ingests ground-level photos directly from device camera with timestamps.
  * Offline Draft Queue: Stores surveys locally in IndexedDB/LocalStorage when working in remote rural areas.
  * Auto-Reconciliation Engine: Automatically uploads and synchronizes pending surveys once internet connectivity resumes.
- Key Files: `src/services/mobile-survey.service.ts`, `src/components/field-survey/MobileFieldSurveyModal.tsx`.

5.5. Module 5: Drone Photogrammetry & Orthomosaic GIS Overlay

7. COMPLETE END-TO-END WORKFLOW & LIFECYCLE
====================================================================================================

The complete operational lifecycle follows an evidence-backed verification pipeline:

Step 1: Farmer Onboarding & 7/12 Land Application
- Farmer registers online via 4-step wizard: Personal Info -> Identity Verification -> Land Details -> Document Upload.
- Enters Survey No, Gat No, Taluk, Village, and declared crop acreage.
- Uploads digital copy of 7/12 land extract.
- System generates application ID (`AGRI-APP-2026-XXXXXX`) and dispatches instant SMS acknowledgement.
- Application status transitions to `PENDING_TAHSILDAR_REVIEW`.

Step 2: Tahsildar Jurisdiction Review
- Application appears in the jurisdictional queue of the assigned Taluka Tahsildar (e.g. Haveli Taluk).
- Tahsildar inspects 7/12 extract against Revenue Department land records.
- Action:
  * APPROVE: Application transitions to `APPROVED`. Automated SMS notifies farmer to upload ground photos.
  * REJECT: Tahsildar must enter mandatory justification. Application transitions to `REJECTED`. Audit log recorded.

Step 3: Farmer Ground Photograph Upload
- Farmer captures 5-6 mandatory ground photographs from distinct viewpoints (North boundary, South boundary, 
  crop rows, irrigation source).
- Photos are tagged with device GPS metadata and uploaded to secure private storage.

Step 4: Drone Inspection Mission Assignment
- Tahsildar or Field Supervisor dispatches an aerial inspection mission to a certified Field Worker / Drone Pilot.
- Field worker travels to the plot, executes RTK drone survey, and captures geotagged nadir & oblique photos.
- Field worker uploads drone imagery. Flight status updates from `SCHEDULED` to `PROCESSED`.
- Georeferenced orthomosaic is processed and aligned with the cadastral parcel boundaries.

Step 5: Side-by-Side Image Comparison Workspace
- Tahsildar opens the Image Comparison Workspace.
- Displays farmer-uploaded ground photos side-by-side with high-resolution drone orthomosaics.
- Synchronized pan/zoom allows micro-inspection of crop rows, boundary stones, and irrigation infrastructure.
- Tahsildar records comparison findings (`CONSISTENT` or `DISCREPANT`).

Step 6: AI Crop Intelligence & Verification
- System executes Random Forest classification (`MH-CROP-RF-v2.3`) using Sentinel-2 L2A spectral bands.
- Ingests latest field surveyor observations and NDVI vigor readings.
- Tahsildar reviews declared crop vs observed crop vs AI prediction.
- Tahsildar formally confirms or amends the crop entry (converting `AI_PREDICTION` into official statutory record).

Step 7: Final Verification Report & Digital Seal
- System compiles the comprehensive Verification Report.
- Generates official, multi-page PDF dossier featuring:
  * Government of Maharashtra seal and header.
  * WGS84 geodesic boundary coordinates and parcel acreage.
  * Farmer identity references and 7/12 extract details.
  * Ground photos and drone orthomosaic thumbnail evidence.
  * Verified crop type, season, and irrigation method.
  * Digital signature of the reviewing Tahsildar and statutory non-title disclaimer.
- Final SMS confirmation dispatched to farmer with download link.

Step 8: Continuous Monitoring & Rapid Disaster Assessment
- System continues monitoring Sentinel-2 STAC feeds and Open-Meteo weather every 5 days.
- In event of severe weather (e.g. unseasonal hailstorm in Baramati), system runs bi-temporal NDVI change detection:
  * Compares pre-event NDVI against post-event NDVI.
  * Computes negative delta (vegetation loss) and delineates affected acreage.
  * Generates Potential Crop-Damage Assessment Report (Report 10) for rapid compensation disbursement.
