1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
====================================================================================================

AgriSurvey AI is a modern, high-precision web platform engineered to modernize agricultural land
administration, crop acreage estimation, and ground verification across Maharashtra. The system
unifies Farmers, Taluk Tahsildars (Revenue Officers), Certified Drone Pilots (Field Surveyors),
and State Decision-Makers onto an integrated, evidence-backed digital platform.

The system replaces error-prone, paper-bound revenue inspections with a tamper-resistant workflow:
  1. Multilingual Farmer Self-Registration with 7/12 land extract validation & Aadhaar-safe privacy.
  2. Ground Photogrammetry submission (mandatory 5–6 georeferenced boundary and crop photos).
  3. High-resolution RTK Drone Aerial Inspections & Georeferenced Orthomosaics.
  4. Tahsildar Dual-Pane Image Comparison Workspace (Side-by-side ground vs. aerial verification).
  5. Multi-temporal Sentinel-2 L2A Satellite Remote Sensing (Earth Search STAC API) with genuine
     7-index spectral band processing (NDVI, EVI, SAVI, NDWI, NDMI, NDRE, GCVI).
  6. Agronomic AI & Machine Learning Classification (GM-ML-V2 LightGBM 158-feature phenology model).
  7. Automated Legal Verification Reports (Official PDF export with digital verification seal & QR code).
  8. Real-time DLT-compliant SMS Notifications (MSG91 Flow API) & bilingual notifications (Marathi/English).

EXECUTIVE VERDICT:
The web application is fully built, tested, and ready for end-to-end stakeholder demonstration and
phased pilot rollout. All 26 core application routes, 4 role-based portals, and dual-mode database
engines (Cloud Supabase PostGIS + Local Durable Simulation) are operational with zero build errors.

      1. NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) — Biomass & canopy vigor.
      2. EVI (Enhanced Vegetation Index) — High-biomass saturation compensation.

      5. NDMI (Normalized Difference Moisture Index) — Crop water stress and internal leaf moisture.
      6. NDRE (Normalized Difference Red Edge) — Chlorophyll activity & nitrogen status.
      7. GCVI (Green Chlorophyll Vegetation Index) — Dense canopy differentiation.
  * Real-time Agrometeorology: Centroid-based live weather feeds via Open-Meteo (Temperature,

4. ARCHITECTURE & SECURITY COMPLIANCE
====================================================================================================

1. DUAL-ENGINE PARADIGM:
   - Production Cloud Mode: Powered by Supabase PostgreSQL 15+ with PostGIS spatial extensions,
     Supabase Storage for confidential documents, and 7 secure Edge Functions.
   - Development & Offline Simulation Mode: When cloud keys are not configured, the website
     transparently switches to a local durable browser engine pre-loaded with realistic Pune
     agricultural records (Wagholi, Baramati, Haveli). This enables flawless offline demonstrations
     to committee members without external internet dependency.

2. AADHAAR & CITIZEN DATA PRIVACY:
   - In accordance with UIDAI and national data security norms, full Aadhaar numbers are NEVER
     stored or transmitted in plaintext.
   - The platform uses masked identity document tokens, EPIC Voter IDs, and PAN references.

3. STATUTORY COMPLIANCE:
   - Every generated document and report includes statutory disclaimers clarifying that agricultural
     crop verification does not constitute judicial title transfer under the Maharashtra Land Revenue
     Code, 1966.

4. BILINGUAL ACCESSIBILITY:
   - Native support for Marathi (मराठी) as the default language for grassroots usability across
     village Talathis and farmers, with immediate switching to English (en) and 8 other Indian languages.
